# Actua Learning

The training website of Actua Business Services Limited: visual tutorials for clients and staff on accounting software, tax, payroll and office tools.

**Open it:** https://connex222.github.io/ACTUA-TUTORIAL/

## What's on it

- **34 courses** in `courses/`, including Xero, Sage, QuickBooks, FreeAgent, IRIS, Staffology and Sage payroll, VAT, MTD for Income Tax, Self Assessment, CIS, Microsoft 365, Teams, Copilot and AI.
- **Learning paths** (`path-*.html`) that group courses for getting started, bookkeeping, tax, payroll, business owners, AI, IRIS and firm staff.
- **My progress** (`progress.html`), saved on your own device.

## Staff courses

13 courses are for staff only. They are encrypted, and open only with the staff password. Ask the office for it; it is never stored in this repository.
