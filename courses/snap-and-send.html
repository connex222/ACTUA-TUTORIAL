    Lesson 1 of 8

    Why receipts matter

    3 minutes

    In this lesson
    • What a receipt proves
    • What a missing receipt costs you

    A receipt or invoice is your proof that a business cost was real. Your accountant uses it to claim the cost against your profits, and to reclaim VAT if you’re VAT-registered.

    Lower tax

    Every genuine business cost you can prove reduces the profit you pay tax on.

    VAT back

    If you’re VAT-registered, you need a valid VAT invoice or receipt to reclaim the VAT.

    Protection

    If HMRC asks questions, receipts are the evidence that backs up your figures.

    A bank statement isn’t a receipt

    A bank or card statement shows that money left your account. It doesn’t show what you bought or how much VAT you paid. It can’t replace a receipt for VAT.

    The good news: you don’t need a shoebox. A clear photo or digital copy is fine, and an app sends it straight to your accountant.

    Lesson 2 of 8

    What makes a good receipt

    5 minutes

    In this lesson
    • The details every receipt needs
    • The £250 rule for VAT
    1. Who you paid: the supplier’s name and address.
    2. Their VAT number: only appears if they’re VAT-registered. You need it to reclaim VAT.
    3. The date.
    4. What you bought: so the cost can be put in the right category.
    5. The VAT rate and amount, if VAT was charged.
    6. The total paid.

    Example receipt. The supplier and VAT number are made up.

    The £250 rule (for VAT-registered businesses)

    Total including VATWhat you need to reclaim VAT
    £250 or lessA till receipt is usually enough, as long as it shows the supplier’s name, address and VAT number, the date, what was bought, the VAT rate and the total. This is called a simplified VAT invoice.
    Over £250Ask for a full VAT invoice. It also shows an invoice number, your business name and address, and the amounts before and after VAT.
    These usually aren’t good enough on their own

    Card machine slips (they show only the amount), order confirmation emails, delivery notes, quotes and pro forma invoices. Always ask for, or download, the proper receipt or invoice.

    No VAT number?

    Some suppliers aren’t VAT-registered, so their receipts have no VAT number. That’s fine: the receipt still proves the cost. There’s just no VAT to reclaim on it.

    4 minutes

    In this lesson

    • What they all have in common

    Your accountant will set you up with one app and tell you which. They all work the same basic way: you photograph or email a receipt, the app reads the details, and it passes them to your bookkeeping software.

    Getting set up

    1. Accept the invitation email from your accountant or the app, and set a strong password.
    2. Install the app on your phone from the App Store or Google Play, and sign in.
    3. Find your personal email-in address in the app’s settings and save it as a contact, such as “Receipts”.

    Lesson 4 of 8

    Snap it: taking a good photo

    4 minutes

    In this lesson
    • Six rules for a readable photo
    • How to handle long and multi-page receipts
    Flat

    Smooth out creases and lay it on a flat surface.

    Bright

    Good light, no shadow from your hand or phone. Avoid flash glare on shiny paper.

    Dark background

    A white receipt on a dark table helps the app find the edges.

    Whole thing

    All four edges in the frame, from the supplier name to the total.

    One at a time

    One receipt per photo, unless the app asks otherwise.

    Check it

    Zoom in before sending. If you can’t read it, nobody else can.

    Long receipts and multi-page invoices

    Most apps let you take several photos and join them into one document. Look for an option such as “multi-page” or “add page” before taking the second photo. Don’t send each page as a separate receipt, or it may be counted more than once.

    Do it straight away

    Till receipts are printed on heat-sensitive paper that fades. A receipt photographed today is readable; the same receipt found in a coat pocket in six months may be blank.

    Lesson 5 of 8

    Send it: emails, PDFs and uploads

    4 minutes

    In this lesson
    • How to send invoices that arrive by email
    • What to do with online-only receipts

    Invoices that arrive by email

    1. Open the supplier’s email.
    2. Forward it to your app’s email-in address, with the invoice attached as a PDF if possible.
    3. If the email only has a link, download the invoice from the link first, then forward or upload the PDF.

    Online shopping and accounts

    Amazon, utility companies, phone providers and software subscriptions keep invoices in your online account. Download the invoice (not the order confirmation) as a PDF and upload or forward it.

    Send the document, not a screenshot

    A PDF downloaded from the supplier is clearer and complete. Screenshots often cut off the VAT number or total.

    Send each receipt once

    If you’ve photographed a receipt, don’t also email it. Duplicates create extra work and can lead to costs being counted twice.

    Lesson 6 of 8

    Add the helpful details

    3 minutes

    In this lesson
    • The short note that saves a phone call

    The app reads the supplier, date and amount. It can’t know why you bought something. Most apps have a notes or description box. A few words there save your accountant a phone call.

    ReceiptHelpful note
    Restaurant bill“Lunch with client, J Smith of Parker Cafe, to discuss contract”
    Supermarket“Cleaning supplies for the shop, not personal”
    Fuel“Van, registration AB12 CDE”
    Hardware store“Materials for job at 22 Mill Lane”
    Mixed receipts

    If one receipt has business and personal items, say so in the note, such as “£12.40 business, rest personal”. Better still, pay for them separately at the till.

    Not every cost is allowable

    Some costs, such as entertaining clients, have special tax rules. Send the receipt anyway with a note; your accountant will decide how to treat it.

    Good habits, and how long to keep records

    4 minutes

    In this lesson
    • A routine that takes seconds a day
    • How long records must be kept
    WhenWhat to do
    At the tillSnap the receipt before you leave the shop or car park.
    When an invoice email arrivesForward it to your email-in address there and then.
    Once a weekCheck your inbox and online accounts for anything missed, and look for messages from your accountant in the app.

    How long to keep records

    Business typeKeep records for at least
    Sole trader or partnership5 years after the 31 January Self Assessment deadline for the tax year
    Limited company6 years from the end of the company’s financial year
    VAT-registered businessVAT records for 6 years
    What about the paper?

    HMRC accepts clear, complete digital copies, so you don’t usually need the paper once it’s captured. Check with your accountant before throwing paper away, especially for large purchases.

    Lesson 8 of 8

    Lost or missing receipts

    4 minutes

    In this lesson
    • How to get a replacement
    • What to tell your accountant
    1. Check your email and online accounts. Many shops email receipts, and online accounts keep invoices.
    2. Ask the supplier for a copy. Most will reprint a receipt or resend an invoice if you give the date, amount and the last four digits of the card used.
    3. If there’s no copy, write a short note of what was bought, where, when and why, and send it with the matching line from your bank statement. Tell your accountant it’s a missing receipt.
    No VAT invoice, no VAT back

    Without a valid VAT invoice or receipt, the VAT on that purchase normally can’t be reclaimed, even if the cost itself can still be claimed. That’s why a replacement is always worth asking for.

    Something’s gone wrong

    Select a problem to see the fix.

    The app keeps reading the wrong amount

    Retake the photo flat, in good light, on a dark background, with the whole receipt in view. If it’s still wrong, most apps let you correct the figure before submitting. Your accountant will also check it.

    My receipt has faded

    Photograph what you can, then ask the supplier for a copy (lesson 8). In future, snap receipts on the day you get them.

    I sent the same receipt twice

    Tell your accountant, or delete the duplicate in the app if it hasn’t been processed yet. Don’t worry; duplicates are common and easy to remove when they’re flagged early.

    I can’t find my email-in address

    It’s in the app’s settings or profile area, often labelled as your submission or upload email. Or ask your accountant to send it to you.

    The invoice is addressed to me personally, not the business

    For purchases over £250, ask the supplier to reissue it in the business’s name, especially if you’re VAT-registered. For smaller items, send it with a note explaining it was for the business.

    Reference

    Words explained

    Email-in address
    Your personal address for forwarding receipts and invoices straight into the app.
    Full VAT invoice
    The complete invoice needed for purchases over £250, showing both businesses’ details and the VAT.
    Input VAT
    VAT you pay on business purchases, which a VAT-registered business can usually reclaim.
    Pro forma invoice
    A request for payment in advance. It isn’t a VAT invoice; ask for the proper invoice afterwards.
    Receipt capture
    Photographing or forwarding receipts so an app can read and file them.
    Simplified VAT invoice
    A till receipt with enough details to reclaim VAT on purchases of £250 or less.
    VAT number
    A supplier’s VAT registration number, starting GB in the UK.
