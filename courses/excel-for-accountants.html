    Cleaning bank and software exports

    5 minutes

    In this lesson
    • Making messy CSV files usable
    ProblemFix
    Extra spaces in descriptions=TRIM(A2) removes extra spaces
    Odd hidden characters=CLEAN(A2) removes non-printing characters
    Numbers stored as text=VALUE(A2), or Data, then Text to Columns, then Finish
    Dates that won’t sortText to Columns with the right date format (DMY), or =DATEVALUE(A2)
    Money in and out in one column=MAX(A2,0) for in and =-MIN(A2,0) for out
    Duplicate rowsData, then Remove Duplicates, but check first (lesson 6)
    Keep the original

    Always keep an untouched copy of the export and do your cleaning on a copy.

    Power Query

    For exports you clean every month, Data, then Get Data (Power Query) can record the cleaning steps and repeat them at the click of Refresh.

    Lesson 3 of 7

    SUMIFS and COUNTIFS

    5 minutes

    In this lesson
    • Totals by category, month or supplier

    SUMIFS adds up values that meet one or more conditions. COUNTIFS counts them.

    FormulaGives
    =SUMIFS(E:E, D:D, "Motor")Total of column E where column D is Motor
    =SUMIFS(E:E, D:D, "Motor", A:A, ">="&DATE(2026,10,1))Motor costs from 1 October 2026
    =COUNTIFS(D:D, "Sales")How many sales lines
    Put criteria in cells

    Instead of typing "Motor" into the formula, refer to a cell holding it, so one formula can be copied down a whole list of categories.

    Lesson 4 of 7

    XLOOKUP

    5 minutes

    In this lesson
    • Looking up values the modern way

    XLOOKUP finds a value in one column and returns the matching value from another. It replaces VLOOKUP in current versions of Excel.

    PartExample
    What to find"S02"
    Where to lookA2:A5 (supplier codes)
    What to returnB2:B5 (supplier names)
    If not found"Not found"

    =XLOOKUP("S02", A2:A5, B2:B5, "Not found")

    Why it beats VLOOKUP

    XLOOKUP can look left, doesn’t break when columns are inserted, matches exactly by default, and handles “not found” without wrapping in IFERROR.

    Older Excel?

    If XLOOKUP isn’t available, use =IFERROR(INDEX(B2:B5, MATCH("S02", A2:A5, 0)), "Not found").

    Lesson 5 of 7

    Pivot tables

    4 minutes

    In this lesson
    • Summaries in seconds
    1. Click anywhere in your data (ideally a Table).
    2. Insert, then PivotTable, then OK.
    3. Drag Category to Rows and Amount to Values. Excel totals each category.
    4. Drag Date to Columns and group by Months to see a month-by-month view.
    5. Right-click and choose Refresh after the data changes.
    Double-click to drill in

    Double-click any total in a pivot table to see the rows that make it up.

    Reconciliation techniques

    5 minutes

    In this lesson
    • Finding differences and duplicates
    TaskTechnique
    Spot possible duplicates=COUNTIFS(A:A,A2, B:B,B2, E:E,E2): a result above 1 means the same date, description and amount appear more than once
    Highlight duplicatesHome, then Conditional Formatting, then Highlight Cells Rules, then Duplicate Values
    Match two listsXLOOKUP or COUNTIF to see which items in one list are missing from the other
    Avoid penny differences=ROUND(A2,2) on calculated amounts before comparing
    Check a totalA difference cell such as =Statement - Ledger that should show 0.00
