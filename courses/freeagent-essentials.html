    What FreeAgent is, and who it’s for

    3 minutes

    In this lesson
    • What FreeAgent does
    • Who can get it free

    FreeAgent is online accounting software built for small businesses: sole traders, partnerships, small limited companies and landlords. It runs in a web browser and has a phone app for iPhone and Android.

    Bank and bookkeeping

    Bank transactions come in automatically and you “explain” what each one was.

    Invoices and expenses

    Send invoices, record bills and claim back money you spent personally.

    Tax Timeline

    Shows upcoming tax deadlines and estimated bills in one place.

    Free with NatWest Group banks

    FreeAgent is owned by NatWest Group. You can usually get it free if the business has a business current account with NatWest, Royal Bank of Scotland or Ulster Bank, for as long as you keep the account. With a Mettle account, it’s free if you make at least one transaction a month through Mettle. Optional add-ons may cost extra.

    Check with us before signing up

    If Actua looks after your books, let us set FreeAgent up with you, so your account is linked to us from the start.

    Lesson 3 of 10

    Getting set up

    4 minutes

    In this lesson
    • The settings to check first
    • What access levels mean
    1. Open Settings (the gear icon) and check your business name, address and contact details. These appear on your invoices.
    2. Add your logo and bank details for invoices, so customers know how to pay you.
    3. If the business is VAT-registered, check the VAT details are entered correctly. Ask your accountant if unsure.
    4. Connect your bank account (lesson 4).

    Access levels

    Everyone who uses FreeAgent gets an access level that controls what they can see and do. For example, you need level 6 or above to explain bank transactions. If a button is missing for you, your access level may be too low; ask the account owner.

    Leave these to your accountant

    Don’t change your accounting year dates, VAT registration date or VAT scheme without speaking to your accountant. FreeAgent uses these dates to build your tax returns and the Tax Timeline.

    Lesson 5 of 10

    Explaining transactions

    6 minutes

    In this lesson
    • Explain a single transaction
    • The explanations that link to invoices, bills and transfers

    “Explaining” is FreeAgent’s word for telling it what a bank transaction was. It’s the same job other software calls categorising or reconciling.

    1. Open the bank account and select a red, unexplained transaction.
    2. Choose the type: for example, a payment, an invoice receipt, a bill payment or a transfer.
    3. For payments, choose the category (such as Telephone or Travel) and check the VAT.
    4. Attach a receipt if you have one.
    5. Select Explain Transaction. It turns green.
    If the money is…Explain it as
    A customer paying an invoice you sent from FreeAgentInvoice receipt, linked to that invoice. Don’t use a sales category, or the sale is counted twice.
    You paying a supplier’s bill already enteredBill payment, linked to that bill.
    Moving money between two of your business accountsTransfer. FreeAgent records equal money out and money in on the two accounts.
    Everything else, such as a card purchaseA payment with the right category and VAT.
    Lots of similar transactions?

    When you explain one, FreeAgent may offer to explain others with similar descriptions in the same way. You can also select several unexplained transactions and explain them in bulk. This doesn’t work for invoice receipts or bill payments, which need linking one by one.

    Lesson 6 of 10

    Guess, and approving its work

    3 minutes

    In this lesson
    • What Guess does
    • Why every guess needs checking

    Guess is a FreeAgent feature that tries to explain new bank feed transactions for you, based on how you’ve explained similar ones before. Its guesses land in the For Approval tab, shown in orange.

    1. Open the bank account and select the For Approval tab.
    2. Check each guess: is the type, category and VAT right?
    3. If it’s right, approve it. If it’s wrong, change it before approving.
    Guesses count straight away

    Guessed transactions already behave like explained ones in your figures, even before you approve them. A wrong guess left unchecked flows straight into your VAT return and tax estimates. Check the For Approval tab every week.

    If Guess keeps getting a supplier wrong, you can switch off that rule, or switch Guess off for a whole bank account, from the Banking area.

    Lesson 7 of 10

    Creating and sending invoices

    5 minutes

    In this lesson
    • Create and send an invoice
    • Why “Mark as Sent” matters
    1. Select the Work tab, then Invoicing.
    2. Select Add New Invoice (or Create your first invoice).
    3. Choose the customer, check the dates and payment terms, and add a line for each thing you’re charging for.
    4. Save it. It starts as a draft.
    5. Email it from FreeAgent, or select Mark as Sent and send it yourself.
    Drafts don’t count

    An invoice only counts in your accounts and VAT return once it’s sent or marked as sent. A forgotten draft means missing sales, and missing VAT on your return.

    When the customer pays

    When the money arrives in your bank, explain the bank transaction as an invoice receipt and pick the invoice. FreeAgent marks the invoice as paid.

    Lesson 8 of 10

    Bills and out-of-pocket expenses

    5 minutes

    In this lesson
    • When to use a bill, an expense or just the bank
    SituationWhere to record it
    A supplier sends an invoice you’ll pay laterWork, then Bills. When you pay it, explain the bank payment as a bill payment.
    You paid for something business-related with your own personal money or cardWork, then Expenses, as an out-of-pocket expense. The business owes you that money back.
    You paid with the business card or accountNothing extra: just explain the bank transaction, and attach the receipt there.
    Don’t record it twice

    If you paid with the business card, don’t also add it as an out-of-pocket expense. That would claim the cost twice, and record that the business owes you money it doesn’t.

    Snap receipts on the go

    The FreeAgent phone app can photograph receipts and attach them. The Snap & Send course in this series covers good receipt habits.

    Lesson 9 of 10

    The Tax Timeline and VAT

    6 minutes

    In this lesson
    • Read the Tax Timeline
    • Check and file a VAT return

    The Tax Timeline

    The Tax Timeline on the Overview screen is a calendar of your important tax dates and payments, built from your accounting and VAT dates. From each item’s three-dot menu you can file online with HMRC (where FreeAgent supports it), mark it as filed, or mark a bill as paid.

    Estimates are only as good as your bookkeeping

    The tax amounts shown are estimates based on what’s in FreeAgent today. Unexplained transactions, guesses awaiting approval and draft invoices all make them less accurate.

    Filing a VAT return

    1. Explain every transaction in the VAT period, approve all guesses, and make sure every invoice is sent or marked as sent.
    2. Select the Taxes tab, then VAT, and open the return that’s due.
    3. Check the figures. The Full Report tab shows the breakdown behind them.
    4. Tick the legal declaration and select Submit VAT Return. FreeAgent sends it to HMRC through its Making Tax Digital connection.
    5. Pay HMRC by the deadline: normally one calendar month and seven days after the period ends.
    If your accountant files for you

    If Actua prepares your VAT returns, Self Assessment or company accounts, don’t file them yourself from FreeAgent. Once a return reaches HMRC, it can’t simply be taken back.

    • A simple routine that keeps your accountant happy

    Reports live under the Accounting tab. The two most useful are:

    ReportAnswers the question
    Profit and LossDid the business make money over this period?
    Balance SheetWhat does the business own and owe on this date?

    Your weekly ten minutes

    1. Explain anything red in Banking.
    2. Check and approve anything orange in For Approval.
    3. Send or mark as sent any draft invoices.

    5. Glance at the Tax Timeline for anything coming up.
    Your accountant can see your FreeAgent

    Help

    Something’s gone wrong

    Select a problem to see the fix.

    I can’t see the Explain button

    You need access level 6 or above to explain bank transactions. Ask the account owner or your accountant to raise your access level.

    A customer has paid but the invoice still shows as unpaid

    The bank transaction was probably explained as a sale or other income instead of an invoice receipt. Open the transaction, change the type to invoice receipt and pick the invoice.

    My sales look too low

    Check Work, then Invoicing, for invoices still in draft. Drafts don’t count until they’re sent or marked as sent.

    Guess keeps getting one supplier wrong

    Correct the guesses, then switch off that Guess rule in the Banking area so future transactions from that supplier stay unexplained for you to explain yourself.

    A transfer appears twice

    When both accounts have bank feeds, a transfer comes in on each side. Explain one side as a transfer and FreeAgent normally explains the other side automatically. If both were explained separately, ask your accountant before deleting anything.

    My bank feed has stopped

    Bank permissions need renewing from time to time. Look for a message in the Banking area and reconnect through your bank’s secure page.

    Reference

    FreeAgent words explained

    Access level
    Controls what each user can see and do. Level 6 or above is needed to explain bank transactions.
    Bill payment
    The explanation type for paying a supplier bill already entered in FreeAgent.
    Draft
    An invoice that hasn’t been sent or marked as sent, so it doesn’t count yet.
    Explain
    Tell FreeAgent what a bank transaction was.
    For Approval
    The tab of transactions Guess has explained, shown in orange, waiting for you to check.
    Guess
    FreeAgent’s feature that tries to explain new transactions automatically.
    Invoice receipt
    The explanation type for a customer paying one of your FreeAgent invoices.
    Out-of-pocket expense
    A business cost paid with your own personal money, which the business owes back to you.
    Tax Timeline
    The calendar of tax deadlines and estimated bills on the Overview screen.
    Unexplained
    A bank transaction FreeAgent doesn’t know about yet, shown in red.
