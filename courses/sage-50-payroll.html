    What Sage 50 Payroll is

    3 minutes

    In this lesson
    • What the software does
    • Why updates matter

    Sage 50 Payroll is desktop payroll software installed on a Windows computer. It works out pay, tax, National Insurance, pensions and statutory pay, produces payslips and sends your submissions to HMRC.

    Install every legislation update

    Sage releases an update before each new tax year with the new rates. You’ll be prompted to download it. Payroll for the new tax year must not be run until it’s installed.

    Learn the rules first

    This course covers how to use the software. The Payroll from Zero course explains the rules behind it.

    Lesson 3 of 8

    Backups: your undo button

    3 minutes

    In this lesson
    • When to back up
    • Where to keep backups
    1. Before each pay run, and again before Update Records, use File, then Backup.
    2. Give the backup a clear name, such as the company and pay date.
    3. Keep copies somewhere other than the payroll computer, such as secure cloud storage, in case the computer fails.
    Restoring overwrites

    Restoring a backup replaces everything entered since. Only restore if you’re sure, and ideally speak to your accountant first.

    Lesson 4 of 8

    Adding a new starter

    4 minutes

    In this lesson
    • Setting up a new employee correctly
    1. Do the right to work check and collect the P45 or starter checklist (see Payroll from Zero).
    2. Select New Employee and work through the screens: personal details, start date, pay frequency and rate.
    3. Enter the starting tax code and previous pay and tax from the P45, if they have one.
    4. Check the pension settings so the employee is assessed for auto-enrolment.
    5. Save. Add starters before opening Enter Payments for their first pay run.
    Starter details go to HMRC

    The new starter information is sent on your next FPS. Typing errors in names, dates of birth or National Insurance numbers cause mismatches at HMRC.

    Lesson 5 of 8

    Running the pay run: five steps

    6 minutes

    In this lesson
    • The pay run in order
    • What to check before updating
    1. Change the process date to the date employees will be paid. Check the status bar shows the right tax month.
    2. Enter Payments. If needed, use Reset Payments first to clear last period’s one-off values. Enter hours and rates, overtime, bonuses and deductions. Check each employee’s tax code, gross pay, tax, National Insurance and net pay.
    3. Payslips and reports. Produce payslips (printed, emailed or online) and run the pre-update reports. Check totals against what you expected.
    4. Update Records. Back up first. This saves the pay run into each employee’s history.
    5. Send the Full Payment Submission to HMRC, on or before the pay date.
    Absences and starters

    Enter sickness or maternity absence before you update records, so statutory pay is included. Starters must be added before you open Enter Payments.

    Don’t send a test FPS

    There’s no such thing as a practice submission. Anything you send is treated as real by HMRC.

    Lesson 6 of 8

    Month end

    4 minutes

    In this lesson
    • The month-end checklist
    1. Run the P32 report. It shows what you owe HMRC for the tax month.
    2. Send an Employer Payment Summary (EPS) if you need to: to claim Employment Allowance, reclaim statutory pay, or report no payments. Due by the 19th.
    3. Pay HMRC by the 22nd (electronic payment).
    4. Send pension contributions to your pension provider on time.

    Lesson 7 of 8

    Leavers

    3 minutes

    In this lesson
    • Processing someone who has left
    1. Pay their final wages, including any untaken holiday, and update records.
    2. Run the Leaver Wizard and enter their leaving date.
    3. Produce their P45 and give it to them.
    4. Send the FPS, which includes their leaving date.
    Leaving date matters

    If you only find out after the final FPS, send the leaving date on the next FPS. Don’t leave people on the payroll who are no longer paid.

    Lesson 8 of 8

    Year end

    4 minutes

    In this lesson
    • Closing the tax year safely
    1. Install the year-end legislation update when prompted. You can still finish the old year without it.
    2. Process the final pay run of the year and send its FPS, marked as the final submission for the year, or send a final EPS.
    3. Check the Last updated column: every current employee should show the final process date of the year.
    4. Back up, then produce P60s and give them to employees by 31 May.
    5. Move into the new tax year in the software, so new rates and tax codes apply.
    Fix problems before moving on

    Once you’re in the new tax year, it’s not always easy to go back and correct last year’s details. Check names, National Insurance numbers and leavers first.

    Help

    Something’s gone wrong

    Select a question to see the answer.

    I updated records with a mistake

    Don’t try to fix it by editing history. If you took a backup just before, restoring it may be simplest; otherwise speak to your accountant about correcting it in the next pay run.

    HMRC rejected my FPS

    Read the error message in the submissions log. Common causes are wrong employer references or employee details. Correct the details and resubmit.

    The status bar shows the wrong tax month

    Stop and change the process date before entering anything.

    I can’t find the update

    Use the Help menu to check for updates, or contact Sage support. Don’t start the new tax year without it.

    Reference

    Words explained

    Enter Payments
    Where each employee’s pay and deductions for the pay run are entered.
    FPS
    Full Payment Submission, sent on or before payday.
    EPS
    Employer Payment Summary, sent by the 19th when needed.
    P32
    Report of what’s owed to HMRC for the tax month.
    Process date
    The date employees are paid in this pay run.
    Update Records
    Saving the pay run into each employee’s history.
