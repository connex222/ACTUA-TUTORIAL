    What Sage Payroll online is

    3 minutes

    In this lesson
    • What it does
    • How it differs from Sage 50 Payroll

    Sage Payroll is Sage’s online (cloud) payroll. It runs in a web browser, so you can use it from any device, and employees can see their payslips and P60s online or in an app.

    Sage Payroll (online)Sage 50 Payroll (desktop)
    Where it runsWeb browser, any deviceInstalled on a Windows computer
    UpdatesApplied automaticallyDownloaded and installed by you
    BackupsHandled by SageYour job
    PayslipsOnline for employees, or emailed or printedPrinted, emailed or uploaded
    Learn the rules first

    This course covers the software. The Payroll from Zero course explains the payroll rules behind it.

    Lesson 3 of 7

    Adding an employee

    4 minutes

    In this lesson
    • Setting up a starter
    • Moving to Sage part-way through a year
    1. Do the right to work check and collect the P45 or starter checklist.
    2. Select Add employee from the dashboard or the Employees area.
    3. Enter personal details, start date, pay frequency and pay.
    4. Enter tax details: the tax code and, from a P45, previous pay and tax this year.
    5. Check pension settings, then save.
    Joining Sage mid-year?

    If you move to Sage Payroll part-way through a tax year, you enter each employee’s year-to-date pay, tax and National Insurance from your old software during your first pay run, before you confirm it. If you start at the beginning of a tax year, you don’t need to.

    Lesson 4 of 7

    Running a pay run

    5 minutes

    In this lesson
    • The pay run from start to finish
    1. From the dashboard, select Process pay run for the right pay frequency and check the pay date.
    2. Enter this period’s changes: hours, overtime, bonuses, deductions and any absences such as sickness or maternity.
    3. Review the summary: each employee’s gross pay, tax, National Insurance, pension and net pay. Check the payslips and reports.
    4. Confirm the pay run. Sage sends the Full Payment Submission to HMRC; check it shows as accepted.
    5. Pay your employees their net pay on the pay date.
    Check before you confirm

    Once a pay run is confirmed and submitted, corrections usually have to be made in a later pay run. Take time over the review step.

    Lesson 5 of 7

    Pensions

    4 minutes

    In this lesson
    • Automatic assessment
    • Sending contributions

    Sage Payroll assesses each employee for auto-enrolment every pay run and works out the contributions.

    1. Set up your pension scheme in Settings or the Pensions area.
    2. Each pay run, Sage assesses employees and enrols those who are eligible.
    3. Send the letters it produces to newly enrolled employees.
    4. Send contribution information to your provider. For some providers, such as Nest and Smart Pension, this can be done directly from Sage.
    5. Record opt-outs as they happen, and pay contributions on time.

    Lesson 6 of 7

    Online payslips

    3 minutes

    In this lesson
    • Inviting employees
    • What they can see
    1. Make sure each employee has an email address in their record.
    2. Invite them to view payslips online.
    3. They sign up and can then see payslips and P60s online or in the mobile app.
    Still give a payslip

    Employees who don’t sign up still need a payslip each payday. Email or print one for them.

    Lesson 7 of 7

    Month end and year end

    4 minutes

    In this lesson
    • The monthly routine
    • Closing the tax year

    Each month

    1. Check what’s owed to HMRC and pay it by the 22nd.
    2. Send an EPS by the 19th if you need to claim the Employment Allowance, reclaim statutory pay, or report no payments.

    At the end of the tax year

    1. Process the final pay run of the year and make sure its FPS is accepted.
    2. Check employees’ details and leavers before the year closes.
    3. Give everyone employed on 5 April a P60 by 31 May. Employees signed up to online payslips can see theirs online.
    Updates are automatic

    Sage updates the online software with the new tax year’s rates for you. Nothing to install.

    Help

    Something’s gone wrong

    Select a question to see the answer.

    My FPS shows as rejected

    Open the submission details to read HMRC’s reason. Common causes are wrong HMRC references in Settings or employee details. Correct them and resubmit.

    I confirmed a pay run with a mistake

    Speak to your accountant. Most corrections are made in the next pay run rather than by undoing the confirmed one.

    An employee can’t log in to see payslips

    Check their email address in their record, and resend the invitation.

    The HMRC amount looks wrong

    Check whether an EPS for Employment Allowance or statutory pay recovery is needed, then compare with your payroll reports.
