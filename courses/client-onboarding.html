    Lesson 2 of 6

    Before you accept the client

    5 minutes

    In this lesson
    • The checks that come first
    1. Customer due diligence: identify and verify the client and any beneficial owners, and risk-assess them (see the AML course). This must be done before any work starts.
    2. Conflicts of interest: check the practice doesn’t act for anyone whose interests clash, such as the other side of a business sale.
    3. Professional clearance: if the client had a previous accountant, write to them asking whether there’s any professional reason not to act, and requesting handover information.
    4. Can we do the work? Check the practice has the skills, capacity and authorisations for what the client needs.
    No work before checks

    It’s tempting to “just get started” when a client is in a hurry. Don’t: starting before due diligence breaks the Money Laundering Regulations.

    Lesson 4 of 6

    Getting authorised with HMRC

    6 minutes

    In this lesson
    • Which method for which tax

    HMRC uses different authorisation routes for different taxes. Most new authorisations now start in the practice’s agent services account.

    TaxHow to get authorised
    Making Tax Digital for Income TaxDigital handshake from the agent services account (a paper 64-8 doesn’t give MTD access)
    VATDigital handshake from the agent services account
    Self AssessmentOnline agent authorisation, or form 64-8
    Corporation TaxOnline agent authorisation, or form 64-8
    PAYEOnline agent authorisation, the client’s business tax account, or form 64-8
    CISOnline agent authorisation, or the client’s business tax account
    High Income Child Benefit ChargeForm CH995
    1. In the agent services account, select Ask a client to authorise you.
    2. Enter the client’s details and get the authorisation request link.
    3. Send the link to the client. They sign in with their Government Gateway details and accept.
    4. Check the authorisation appears in the agent services account.
    Clients who can’t go online

    If a client is digitally excluded, still create the request, then give them the link and HMRC’s number. HMRC’s Extra Support Team can talk them through it by phone.

    Limited authorisation

    HMRC no longer accepts form 64-8 for limited authorisation requests.

    Lesson 6 of 6

    The first 90 days

    3 minutes

    In this lesson
    • Making a strong start
    1. Send a welcome pack: who to contact, how to use the portal, and key dates.
    2. Send an information request with clear deadlines.
    3. Put every deadline into Practice Management.
    4. Book a check-in after the first piece of work to ask how it’s going.
    Client courses

    Point new clients to the client tutorials in this series, such as OpenSpace, Snap & Send and Self Assessment Survival Guide.
